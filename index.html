Four kinds of people use it

🧑‍🍳

Business owner

Signs up, adds products, picks a look, publishes, handles orders, pays monthly.

dashboard.storeease.in
🧑‍💼

Staff

Helps the owner. Sees only what their role allows (orders only, catalogue only…).

name.storeease.in/dashboard
🛍️

Shopper

Browses a store, logs in with an emailed code, orders. One StoreEase account works in every shop. Pays the store directly (COD/UPI).

name.storeease.in
🛡️

StoreEase team

Sees every business, suspends abuse, tracks revenue (MRR), handles setup requests.

admin.storeease.in

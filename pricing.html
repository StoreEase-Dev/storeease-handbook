Plans & billing

Owners pay StoreEase monthly by auto-pay (UPI AutoPay or a card mandate) through Razorpay. Shoppers never pay through StoreEase: the store collects from them directly.

A subscription over time

Every new store starts with a free 14-day trial. A billing "sweep" in the API checks every store regularly and sends the reminders.

Cancelling and add-ons

Cancel: the store runs to the end of the paid month (reminder 3 days before), then closes. No refunds for part months.

Add-on on/off: an approved auto-pay can't change amount, so the owner approves a new one. Turning on mid-month pays the remaining days upfront.

Two billing modes

The app decides by itself, based on whether Razorpay keys are set.

Running today

Instant mode (no keys)

Subscribe activates Chai for a month at once with a "manual" invoice. It renews itself every month, so testers are never cut off. Add-ons switch instantly. Nothing is charged. Billing shows a "test mode" banner.

Ready, not yet tested for real

Razorpay mode (keys set)

Real auto-pay, webhooks drive every change, the sweep handles trials, failures and cancellations. Going live = put the live keys in AWS and redeploy the backend. Nothing else.

Before testing real paymentsThe Razorpay test account has Subscriptions switched off: Razorpay answers 401 on plans and subscriptions. Ask Razorpay to enable it, then follow the checklist in storeease-backend/docs/billing-setup.md.

How a payment flows through the system

Money rules

Paise, always

Every amount is a whole number of paise (₹499 = 49,900). Formatted only for display.

No GST yet

Invoices say so while tax is 0. When GST starts, set the legal name and GSTIN in the backend settings.

Gapless invoice numbers

One global sequence, INV-…, for every invoice.

The business

The product

A low-cost Shopify alternative for India. Built for people who sell from home on Instagram and WhatsApp, who need a real shop link without hiring an agency.

This is the path the end-to-end tests walk every time.

Login picks up where you left offPressing Login on storeease.in while signed in goes straight to the dashboard of the last business used. Sign out forgets it. Only the shop's public name is remembered in the browser, never a token.

Pages for shops that aren't open

A visitor never sees a bare error. These pages share the homepage's style: a flippable logo switch, a joke, and the footer basics.

closed

Suspended by an admin or by billing. Says "temporarily closed"; never shows the reason or owner links.

coming soon

A new, unpublished shop. Links the owner to Login.

An order's journey

One step at a time. Delivered and cancelled are final; nothing is ever hard-deleted.

Paying and after-sale

StoreEase never holds or moves a shopper's money. The shop is paid, and refunds, by itself.

How a shopper pays

ChooseAt checkout: Cash on delivery or Pay to the shop's UPI, whichever the shop switched on
→
PayUPI: a QR code (desktop) or "Pay with UPI app" (phone) with the exact amount and the note Order 1042 · Priya Pickles filled in
→
"I've paid"One tap. The order shows Payment to confirm to the shop
→
Payment receivedThe shop checks its UPI app and taps this. Only then is the order paid

Cash-on-delivery orders become paid automatically when the shop marks them delivered. The shop's UPI ID is not in the shop's public data; it appears only inside the pay link of that shopper's own order.

Returns and replacements

The owner switches Return and Replacement on per product (both off by default) and sets two windows (days after delivery, 7 by default). A request covers one or more items of one delivered order, one reason, and 1 to 5 photos.

Refunds go where the shopper says

A UPI ID or a bank account (holder, account number, IFSC). No cash, because shopper and shop may be in different cities. If the money doesn't arrive the shopper taps "I haven't received it" and the shop is alerted.

Privacy rules

  • Bank details and photos are personal data: the account number is masked for staff with Returns "view" and shown in full only to "manage" and the owner
  • Photos live in a private bucket, shown through links that last 5 minutes
  • 90 days after a request is final, photos and refund details are deleted
  • Emails never carry bank details

What an owner gets

📦 Catalogue

  • Products with photos, categories, featured items
  • Shop links are made from the name; nobody types one
  • Stock count per product
  • CSV import (with example rows), up to 500 rows

🧾 Orders

  • Status tabs, search, dates
  • Email on every new order, saying how the shopper pays
  • Payment to confirm: UPI orders wait for "Payment received"
  • "Only N left" protection: never oversells

↩️ Returns

  • Choose per product: return, replacement, or neither
  • Approve or reject, record the refund, restock
  • Staff area: none / view / manage

👥 Customers

  • Only people who ordered here; how often, total spent (delivered only)
  • Private notes, CSV export
  • Block an abusive customer

📊 Analytics

  • Money received vs expected, orders, customers
  • Best sellers, busiest hours, low stock
  • Always compared with the period before

🔐 Staff & roles

  • Invite staff, give each one role
  • Presets: Manager, Shop assistant, Packer
  • No role = sees nothing

🎨 Look & address

  • 3 templates, own colours, font, logo (colours suggested from the logo)
  • Bigger preview before publishing; your logo shows in the dashboard sidebar
  • Own domain (e.g. shop.priya.com)

📒 Inventory add-on

  • Record sales from Instagram, WhatsApp, in person
  • Approvals for staff sales
  • Stock history ledger

⭐ Ratings

  • Stars only, 1 to 5
  • Only after a delivered order (verified purchase)
  • The store can't edit or hide them

💳 Billing

  • Plan, usage meters, invoices as PDF
  • Auto-pay by UPI or card

A shopper has one StoreEase account: the same name, phone and saved addresses at every shop. Login is still an emailed code on each shop's own address, and shoppers are told so ("Your details are saved with StoreEase and work in any StoreEase shop"). There is no guest checkout. What the StoreEase team sees

Businesses

Search every store, see plan, add-ons, orders and revenue. Suspend (with a reason the owner is emailed), reactivate, delete (soft, restorable), switch add-ons on.

Platform analytics

MRR and ARR, new / expansion / churn, trial conversion by signup month, top businesses, stores at risk (no orders in 30 days, or orders halved).

Setup requests

Owners who bought done-for-you setup (₹1,299): their notes and photo link, a 48-hour target, Start / Mark live / Cancel.

Out of scope for now

Platform health dashboards, WhatsApp notifications, written reviews, taking shoppers' money through StoreEase (card or UPI collection). See why →

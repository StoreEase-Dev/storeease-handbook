Log in and sign up with Google.

Owners and staff get a Continue with Google button on the login and signup pages at dashboard.storeease.in. No password to make up, no email code to type: Google has already checked the address. Everything that works today keeps working.

Safety

What Google shares

  • Only name, email and profile photo (openid email profile).
  • No access to Gmail, Drive or contacts, and nothing posted anywhere.
  • No Google password ever reaches StoreEase.

What StoreEase checks

  • The token is signed by Google, issued for StoreEase, still valid, and the email is verified.
  • Linking an existing account sends an email, and removing Google needs a password first, so nobody gets locked out.
  • The same rate limits as password login.

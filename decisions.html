Open items

Things someone has to decide or do.

needs a decision

Shopper online payment

StoreEase's Razorpay account with Route payouts to stores (one KYC, StoreEase is merchant), or each store's own Razorpay keys (more onboarding per store)? Decide after beta.

needs a decision

Homepage vs app plans

Decided for the app: Chai ₹499 / Samosa ₹999 / Full Thali ₹1,499, and "Bun maska" = the ₹199 inventory add-on. The homepage was kept as it is: "chai + bun maska" there means ₹499 + ₹1,299 done-for-you setup. Decide how the two should line up.

needs a decision

Trial length in the docs

The backend runs a 14-day trial (Q48). The frontend log (FQ25) mentions a "7-day trial". Confirm 14 + 7 days' grace and fix FQ25.

to do

Razorpay end to end

Ask Razorpay to enable Subscriptions on the test account, add the keys in SSM, run the checklist in billing-setup.md.

to do

SES production access

SES is built (Terraform, DKIM, bounce topic). Re-run bootstrap/, apply, deploy, then ask AWS to lift the sandbox. Until it is granted, only verified addresses get mail.

later

Bounces back into the app

Bounces and complaints reach an email address through SNS, but the app does not read them yet, so it will keep mailing a dead address.

small ask

Upload size limit

A presigned photo upload enforces no maximum size. Add one before opening to the public.

later

Erase a shopper

One action that removes a person from every shop (permanent erasure is deferred, next-phase.md).

to do

Custom domains end to end

Verification and routing are built. Missing: HTTPS per domain, automated with CloudFront's SaaS features (backend #108, infra#3).

to do

Stop the old homepage server, delete the unused Route 53 zone, and revoke the old gh_token.

to do

Homepage contact details

WhatsApp, email and Instagram are placeholders in home.json. Launch target: 31 Dec 2026.

to do

Prove 25 users at once with several API workers.
